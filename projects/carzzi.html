Carzzi

Carzzi is a vehicle-services platform that connects vehicle owners with service providers for maintenance, repairs, and on-demand car care. It spans native mobile apps for customers and service partners plus a web dashboard for operations, with transactional SMS and OTP handled through MSG91.

  3. 03
