Overview

A donation and membership platform for a UK temple organisation, built to comply with HMRC Gift Aid rules. Donors can give one-off or recurring donations and declare Gift Aid; administrators manage members, events, and the compliant reports required for government submissions.

  2. 02

  3. 03

    Developed the Express.js API and MongoDB data model for donations, members, and events.

    Deployed and configured the application on Azure.
