Brand Identity & Packaging

A Botanical Skincare Concept

Self-initiated concept · SOLVÉ is a fictional brand, not a client project

  • Project typeSelf-initiated concept
  • IndustrySkincare & Beauty
  • ToolsAffinity
  • RoleBrand & Packaging Design

Overview

SOLVÉ is a self-initiated concept for a fictional premium skincare brand, inspired by botanical simplicity and modern minimalism. It is not a commissioned client project; the brand, product, and packaging are my own creation.

The brief was my own: imagine a premium skincare brand inspired by botanical simplicity and modern minimalism, then design the logo and one hero product around it.

Brand identity

A wordmark with one living line

The logo is set in a refined serif in capitals, with generous spacing and high stroke contrast for a calm, premium voice. A single fine botanical line rises from the O and opens into a leaf over the V, so the nature reference lives inside the lettering instead of sitting beside it.

Typography
Elegant serif capitals; open, unhurried spacing.
Botanical detail
One continuous leaf stroke, with the accent echoing the same organic gesture.
Personality
Calm, refined, natural, and quietly minimal.

Color palette

  • Forest Green #263D32 Wordmark & type
  • Sage #DCE3D3 Soft supporting tone
  • Warm Ivory #F5F0E6 Backgrounds & label
  • Muted Terracotta #B77B58 Restrained accent

Proposed brand specification. These values are not sampled from the logo artwork.

Packaging design

Daily Refresh Balancing Serum

  • Amber glass dropper bottle
  • White / warm ivory label
  • Forest-green SOLVÉ wordmark
Hierarchy
The wordmark leads, the product name follows in smaller wide-spaced capitals, and a light descriptor closes the stack.
Contrast
Deep green on a clean label reads clearly, and the warm amber glass frames it.
Typography
A serif wordmark paired with a spaced sans for the product name, so the brand and the product read as two voices.
Presentation
Generous empty label space keeps the pack minimal and premium.
