Nehwe

Design study · no public website

Nehwe is a gamified e-learning app that turns lessons into levels, streaks, and rewards to keep learners engaged. The engagement was a ground-up UI/UX project: research, information architecture, custom illustration, and a full design system handed off to development. There is no public website for Nehwe.
